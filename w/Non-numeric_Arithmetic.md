# Non-numeric Arithmetic
Arithmetic applied informally and loosely to concepts, such as:
- graphical objects, such as icons
- actions and verbs
- adjectives

## Examples
- Isotype + Logotype = Imagotype. Usually a simple [concatenation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concatenation) or partial overlay, so it's a sum.
- Isotype * Logotype = Isologo. Hard or impossible to decompose/factorize, so it's a product, not a sum.
- France + food + popular = baguette
